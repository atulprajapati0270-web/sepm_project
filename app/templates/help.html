Help / FAQ

How do I submit a complaint?

Log in, open Complaints, choose a category, enter the problem details and submit. The system generates a complaint ID and administrators can update its status.

How do I report a lost item?

Log in, open Lost & Found, select Lost or Found, add the description and location, then submit.

Are emergency services provided by this application?

No. The system displays verified emergency information and may connect to external services where authorized. Actual emergency response is handled by the relevant official service.
